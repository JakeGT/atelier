# Audit: the screens added on 2026-10-10

What was looked at: the Content page (wording, and pictures and files), the
editor with its History, Wording and Pictures tabs, studio settings (general,
usage), and the starting-point gallery. Each at desktop and phone width, in the
light and the dark theme, with a script that reports anything scrolling
sideways or any control outside the screen, and by eye.

| Screen | What was wrong | Rule | What was done |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Content page, phone | No way back to the editor: the bar hides it at that width | Every screen can be reached and left by a labelled control | A "Back to the editor" link at the top of the page on small screens |
| Content page | The notice about text that cannot be edited was hidden whenever the notice about unconnected wording showed | Say what is true; do not let one message hide another | Both are shown |
| Preview bar (earlier the same day) | Choosing Phone shrank the bar and hid the way back to Desktop | A control is never inside the thing it changes the size of | The bar sits above the frame; tools wrap to a second row |
| Content page (earlier the same day) | Raw JSON for older content; nothing said where a piece appeared | No stored format is shown; everything says what it is and where it goes | Fields for any shape; "Show me where"; "Not on the website yet" |

Nothing scrolled sideways and no control was cut off in any of the twenty-four
combinations checked.
